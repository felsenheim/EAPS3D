6. Competence Contrast (see also section 4)

When the folded layers form broader folds, this often coincides with sequences composed of white, blocky layers with consistent thickness and different composition.

Example from Sample 001: the white and darker layers tend to have similar thickness throughout the fold. However, the darker layers and whiter layers show differences—whiter layers often have thicker hinges and thinner limbs.

In the bottom left, a folded white layer is also fractured, where the fracture offsets the fold along a "tight-latecore" movement.

These observations suggest that the darker layers are less competent than the whiter layers.
